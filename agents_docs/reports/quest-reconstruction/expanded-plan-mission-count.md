# Mission count for the expanded closure plan

**148 verified missions out of 1,153 mapped plan IDs (12.84%).** The detailed first 13 entries remain at 139/167. The first 31 entries, through the Crimson Dofus, have 139 verified missions out of 466 mapped IDs (29.83%). The expanded figure adds nine independently verified Rii missions. Entries with unresolved scope are marked below.

## Counting basis

- The original thirteen lines retain their selected paths and mutually exclusive alternatives are represented once. In Bilbyza, 1510 and 1471 represent the two unresolved alternative slots. Frigost starts after 1111 as the plan specifies.
- For follow-up lines, the selected native categories provide a finite definition inventory. Distinct alternate IDs inside those groups are retained for audit. These totals measure ID coverage, and route-level counts can change once the prerequisites and alternatives are resolved.
- Each ID is assigned to its earliest matching line. The later archaeology entry excludes the missions already assigned to line 4, and the hunter entry excludes the two quests already credited under the Astrub tour. Scenario IDs, objectives and rewards are outside this count.
- Verification requires existing recorded completion evidence under the same acceptance scope as the closed lines. A zero in the verified column means this evidence review credited no completed IDs for that entry. Prior publication and isolated component proofs remain recorded separately, including Frigost 1112’s dialogue test with staged prerequisites.
- The level-201 Grampar, Tundra and related arcs belong to Whispering Kingdom. The 5053–5058 branches belong to Ereboria. Both are mapped under regional follow-up line 35. Shukrute line 29 retains the five distinct regional arcs in categories 847–851. [Whispering Kingdom regional quests](https://methodwakfu.com/quetes/annexes/quetes-royaume-chuchoku-mdc/) · [Shukrute regional chains](https://methodwakfu.com/quetes/principales/eliocalypse-chapitre-5/)
- Ecaflipus, unclassified regional or account missions, environmental quests without a mapped ID, and release reconciliation still need specific mappings. Calamar remains to be mapped. Unmapped entries do not have an estimated count.
