# Manual review: cosmetics and companions

Reviewed 2,353 references across 45 group/type/rarity/acquisition cohorts. This is a design and catalog review, not proof of runtime cosmetic unlock functionality. Prices are kamas per item. The table records every cohort reviewed, its complete level and price range, median, and representative endpoints.

## Design judgement

Approve the draft prices without per-reference overrides. Permanent cosmetic unlocks, companions and mounts reasonably cost much more than single-use food. Emotes remain the accessible entry at 40,000–63,000; titles 120,000–300,000; costumes 98,000–630,000; appearance skins 220,000–380,000; finishers 1,200,000. Companions at 91,000–380,000 and mounts at 870,000–2,000,000 make a coherent separate utility progression. Cosmetics do not approach the true Dofus ceiling of 10,000,000.

Unknown acquisition premiums are explicit shop policy, not proof of exclusivity, scarcity, or absence of a recipe/drop. Current catalog rarity drives the modest differences inside appearance groups; I found no defensible evidence for changing an individual rarity or assigning a special exclusivity premium. Level 0/1 is valid for these groups and the effective pricing defaults are intentional, rather than evidence of incomplete items. For mounts this creates a deliberate inversion: level-1 placeholder Montura tofu (24161) uses default 80 and costs 1,700,000, versus level-35 common Dragopavo at 870,000. Accept as a provisional policy consequence, not a claim that the tofu is harder to acquire.

## Individual exceptions

- ADMIN aura 29879 (470,000), T-pose sprints 30724 and 32913 (290,000 each): retain existing publication holds; numeric prices must not enable sale.
- Add publication review holds for staff-role titles 14111 (Programador de WAKFU, 250,000) and 32461 (Producer WAKFU T5, 250,000). Their explicit role names justify reviewing public availability; do not inflate price or silently remove them. Ankamascota 32847 alone does not establish a staff role and receives no hold.
- Aura 18300 Decocción del tormento and 18713 Brebaje fanático both cost 2,000,000: each has a verified 0.001 drop fraction, mythic quality and level 100/125. 18713 hits the aura cap. These are native type 710 (aura/light), so their beverage-like names do not make them curative food. Retain the permanent-cosmetic pricing pending ordinary runtime acceptance.
- Aura 18299 Efluvios de Stasis (1,300,000) also has 0.001 drop fraction but rare rather than mythic quality. Its lower price is coherent.
- Legendary Milubo feRLZ refs 14422, 19724, 19725, 19726: 2,000,000 each is consistent across the variants. The acquisition source is unresolved; no additional exclusivity premium is justified.
- Montura tofu 24161: retain 1,700,000 under the documented default-level policy explained above.
- Dofus-named references reviewed individually: title 11431 (160,000); costume 26739 (380,000); auras 29764, 29789, 31388, 32355, 33396 (470,000 each); emotes 31504, 31580, 32055, 32393, 32455, 32608 (51,000 each). These are titles, costume, auras or emotes, not actual Dofus. None receives the actual Dofus 10-million price.
- No scoped item has a craft-cost cap or recipe requiring a manual ingredient-cost override. Native type 560 merkasako skins are appearances, distinct from functional furniture.
