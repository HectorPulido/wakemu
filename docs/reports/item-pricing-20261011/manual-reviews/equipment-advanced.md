# Advanced equipment and Dofus manual price review

Reviewer: equipment-advanced. Scope: 5,781 references, equipment native rarities 3/4/5/6/7/10 plus all six Dofus references. Review evaluates price proposals only; no live shop, server or database mutation.

## Economic judgement

Approved with 49 explicit price corrections and three publication holds. Dofus references 20720, 24110, 28417, 31361, 32351 and 33395 remain 10,000,000 kamas each, including the two distinct Prismatic variants. Their fixed premium is a requested policy choice, not a computed drop valuation. They are equipment emblems, not fragments or decorative Dofus objects.

Mythic and legendary cohorts have a credible early-to-endgame progression: level and rare conditional acquisition increase price; craft can be cheaper when ingredients support it. Epic and relic rarity correctly carry larger premiums; souvenir rarity 6 is not treated as epic. Native ADMIN rarity 10 is ineligible rather than a cheap quality tier. Source-unresolved premiums are provisional scarcity policy, not proof an item has no drop or recipe.

The 49 corrections prevent absent-source metadata from making stat-equivalent relic variants cheaper than known rare-drop/craft originals. Core-effect comparison ignores effect identity IDs and the extra generic-400 trigger only for economic comparison; it does not establish native equivalence or authorize merging. Bound-on-account variants receive the same combat-utility floor; resale discount is deliberately not introduced without an explicit bound-shop policy. Other repeated names retain distinct prices when level, rarity or core effects differ. Epic unknown-source versions are often more expensive than verified-drop peers; that is consistent with the requested unavailable-source premium and is retained.

Espada de Valdain 30703 (level 230 relic, 1,400,000) is not forced to match other high-level relics: its 1,144,100 ingredient estimate supports its cheaper craft route. Dora N.I.O.19432 at 5,000,000 is supported by 4,274,080 ingredients; variant24103 receives the same floor. El portal29455 at 4,600,000 has a cheaper verified drop route than its 5,117,400 ingredient estimate, so the craft cost does not override its drop price.

Espada eterna26593 is held: a 14,000,000 ingredient basket cannot fit below the absolute user price cap while preserving crafting economics. Sandalias tramadas29295 has ingredients6,587,480 and price7,500,000: acceptable under the equipment cap, with the service margin slightly compressed by the cap. High-level conditional-drop relics at7,500,000 retain a deliberate plateau below Dofus10,000,000; no false precision is added among capped peers.

The low-level named/event gear at level1 is not automatically deleted; pricing review cannot prove it is incomplete. Generic Anillo (reliquia)32238 and Anillo (épico)32230 are retained provisionally because generic naming alone is insufficient evidence of a beta object. Their unavailable-source prices4,500,000 and3,600,000 remain within their level230 tiers.
