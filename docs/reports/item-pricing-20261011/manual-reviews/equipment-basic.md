# Manual price review: basic equipment and utility gear

Reviewer: equipment-basic. Snapshot: prices.csv, wakemu-prices-v1. Prices are kamas per single item. No database, service or shop publication changes.

## Coverage and judgment

Reviewed 4,973 scoped rows through 167 complete native-type / rarity / acquisition cohorts. Every cohort below was assessed using level span, min/median/max and named min/median/max examples. Also screened all flags and individually examined category-cap violations, missing shop ingredients, unusable recipe flags and all 19 local price inversions greater than 2x within ten levels in the same type/rarity/source. This is cohort review plus targeted individual review, not a claim that 4,973 game objects were individually play-tested.

Common starter gear around 2,500–5,900 and rare endgame drops around 400,000–790,000 are proportionate to a 10,000,000 Dofus ceiling. Unusual legacy equipment with unresolved sources costs more than similar craft/drop items; accept the stated scarcity policy but do not treat missing source data as verified exclusivity. Bags 3,000–280,000 are reasonable utility purchases; capacity and profession restrictions are not modeled and could warrant future refinements. Level-zero pet cosmetics use a default utility level, not a malformed equipment level. Their 65,000/86,000 tiers and modern accessory 59,000–270,000 tiers are reasonable below pet/mount costs.

- Retain hold on admin-quality tool 30840 El buen pico (4,200); do not confuse with ordinary mythic tool 10611 of the same name (380,000).
- Retain 58 drop-priced objects below modeled shop ingredient costs: the drop is a legitimate alternative to crafting. Examples 16761 Armadura de gelatina de menta (91,000 versus 152,220 materials), 15951 Coraza polar (150,000 versus 203,940), and 26766 Corazarza (230,000 versus 285,350). Buying an output below the cost of another route is not itself arbitrage; no resale profit has been established.
- Individually considered the five craft objects with unavailable shop ingredients: 22426 Yelmo inmemorial, 22430 Hombrapagadas, 27411 Lama Larracha, 27414 Anillo Stálgico, 27417 Capa de jalatranki. Retain provisional prices: absence from this shop is not absence from the world.
- Screened all 40 recipe-present-but-unusable entries. Retain unresolved-source premium rather than fabricate craft costs, including 21717 Hydromuleto (610,000), 20948 Botas de Nuovoleguas (600,000), 26396 Capa de sakuya and 26397 Dora namigo (9,300 each). These are explicitly low-confidence acquisition estimates.
