# Manual price review: furniture and miscellaneous

Reviewer: `furniture-misc-manual-review`. Reviewed 2987 references in 190 type/rarity/acquisition cohorts. This is a human-in-the-loop agent review of policy, complete cohort summaries and individually inspected extremes, not a claim of playtesting every object.

## Decisions

- Retain ordinary furniture prices: known portable furniture mappings were filtered in the earlier inventory audit; routine decorations at 6,100–8,200 kamas are acceptable. Crafted furniture can cost more when verified recipes justify it.
- Retain Haven Gem prices (67,000–270,000): room utility supports a premium and gem transfer code exists. Retain station prices (63,000–300,000): repeatable crafting utility and 42 recipes support that range.
- Hold all six Kitea references 11079–11083 and 14207 at provisional 340,000: the model does not distinguish capacity tiers. Do not invent a tier premium without capacity evidence.
- Currency-token floors are denomination-based and conservative; these items remain held because a displayed amount does not prove actual payout.
- Containers stay priced provisionally and held until their contents/opening paths are known. In particular no 1,000,000-kama blanket container price is proposed.
- Hold quest and world-building items for eligibility validation; do not delete their native records.
- Hold every below-input-cost item for recipe/cap reconciliation rather than claim its low capped price is economically reviewed.
