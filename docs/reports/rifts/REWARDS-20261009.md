# Rift item rewards — local DEV policy

Source implementation: `maps/rift_rewards.py`; migration `095_rift_item_rewards.sql`.
This change has not been deployed or applied to the running database. It does not
restart services. Rates and pools are explicit experiment balance, not official
Wakfu drop rates. XP and kamas remain zero.

## Default policy

The same independent reward calculation applies to each eligible original party
member who wins. At least one actual cleared wave and one actual defeated monster
are required. Reached-wave display counters do not increase entitlement.

| Pool | One roll per actual cleared waves | Success probability | Quantity on success | Native allowlist |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Equipment | 2 | 75% | 1 | 2021 Amuleto de jalató; 2022 Anillo de Boz Layt Yer; 2023 Jalabotas |
| Enchantment shards | 1 | 100% | 2–5 | 27083 Esquirlas |
| Sublimations | 4 | 25% | 1 | 27111–27114; native enabled BASIC socket patterns |
| Learnable costumes | 5 | 20% | 1 | 9727 Traje de guarda; 10632 Uniforme de Los que Caminan; 12176 Traje de padrino |

Each pool chooses uniformly among its allowlist; quantity is uniform within its
configured bounds. A pool makes at most 64 rolls per member. These values, minimum
eligibility and pool intervals/allowlists are editable in
`settings.rift_reward_policy`. Losses, aborted runs and capacity closures give
no rewards (`losses: none`). Unsupported policy kinds fail before ownership changes.

Appearances are real learnable inventory items, with their native account binding.
Receiving one does not unlock any appearance. The recipient uses the physical item
through the existing native learning action; successful learning consumes that
item and adds only its own appearance. Virtual socket effect references such as
27094 are rejected as physical loot. Enchantment rewards allow native shards and
verified enabled BASIC sublimation patterns; unsupported special rune classes
remain rejected. Awarding a sublimation does not newly implement its combat effects.

## Durability and delivery

Settlement locks the canonical run and compares actual durable wave/kill counters.
The dispatcher supplies the winning frozen party subset, excluding withdrawals;
observers cannot join this entitlement. Original durable combat participant
ownership and current session leases fence inventory writes.

The policy, actual counters, eligible members, chance draws, allowlist selection
draws and quantity draws are persisted in the immutable combat drop award snapshot.
Every attempt also receives the existing combat drop attempt receipt. The existing
`combat_drop_delivery.apply` then merges compatible stacks, places new native
items or retains overflow in the existing temporary combat inventory. Binding,
UID allocation and native element selections follow the existing item authority.
A failed delivery rolls back both random decisions and ownership in the settlement
transaction. Committed settlement replay does not reroll or duplicate items.

`rift_item_reward_receipts` maps each rift recipient to the native delivery batch.
FightReport loot entries and ordinary inventory/temporary-inventory update packets
are read from that durable batch. Ordinary monster drop resolution remains
suppressed for rifts, preventing a second unrelated payout.

## Automated verification

`test_rift_rewards` covers actual-progress eligibility, loss/empty-progress denial,
roll bounds, malformed policy/draw rejection, durable replay after reopening,
full-bag temporary storage, rollback, spectator/early-win/virtual-effect rejection,
native costume binding and physical learning. A full-default-pool settlement test
checks equipment, shards, sublimations and costumes together and replays settlement.
The focused suite passed **9/9 tests** (three pure and six PostgreSQL). All PostgreSQL tests use disposable schemas in the explicit isolated test database.

## Manual acceptance after a later authorized complete deployment

1. Use a normal eligible party to enter and complete a classic rift. Clear at least
   five waves and survive the configured turn budget. The result must show the
   actual cleared waves/kills and each member's own awarded physical loot.
2. Expect five shard rolls (2–5 shards each); equipment has two 75% rolls,
   sublimations one 25% roll and costumes one 20% roll. The latter pools may
   legitimately award nothing. No observer or withdrawn member receives loot.
3. Repeat with the recipient's bags full. Awards must remain in the native
   temporary loot inventory and be claimable after freeing a slot.
4. Reconnect before claiming overflow. Quantities and item UIDs must remain stable;
   claiming transfers existing items once rather than granting a second award.
5. If a costume drops, use its normal learn action. Only that costume unlocks and
   the physical source item disappears. Reconnect to verify persistence.
6. Lose another run or leave before combat. It must grant no rewards. Ultimate
   rifts remain disabled until their native key transaction is implemented.

For a deterministic full-system manual probe, a maintainer can temporarily use a
single isolated DEV policy with every pool's `chance_bp=10000` and `every_waves=1`,
then restore the saved policy. Such a live database edit is not part of this source
change and has not been performed here.
