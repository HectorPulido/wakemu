# Family arena progression and forbidden-role review

The previous eight open-arena and four final-arena artifacts remain frozen.
They were source-only preparations and have not been activated by these tools.

## Open arenas, role-filtered version 6

`prepare_family_open_dungeons_v2.py` generates eight configurations with the
shared native role policy. Dominants, archmonsters and unknown roles are excluded;
only identified final bosses and ordinary native family members are eligible.
Native metadata properties, rather than display names, determine role.

The single encounter uses native minimum spawn levels and records the shared
composition metric. A single encounter has no cross-room difficulty comparison.
These remain explicitly local single arenas, not claims of official multi-room
progression. Source results: `FAMILY-OPEN-CANDIDATES-V2-20261010.json`.

## Previous final arenas excluded from progressive completion

`FAMILY-FINAL-ARENA-CANDIDATES-V2-20261010.json` reclassifies four prior simplified
arenas as `simplified_pending_not_progressive`. They must not be counted as
completed progressive multi-room dungeons.

- Necromundo and Supervillains have boss-only native dungeon pools. Intermediate
  encounters cannot be filled with ordinary members without new evidenced family
  references; repeating the final bosses in earlier rooms is prohibited.
- Riktus Elite and Hammalmejil have cyclic gate geometry without a proved mandatory
  directed frontier for a safe ordered encounter sequence. The prior all-open,
  empty-intermediate-area fallback does not satisfy progressive multi-room play.

Their original configurations remain available as historical evidence, but no
new progressive configuration or activation was produced for these four.
