# Dungeon expansion blockers — 2026-10-10

All 156 installed definitions were audited. These are offline preparations, not activation. No client files, capture hooks, combat engine or spell engine were changed.

Sixteen physical configurations (eight old plus eight new) pass native identity, combat formation, literal entry/final exterior exit binding, and directed exploration sequence checks. Ten teleport configurations already passed PostgreSQL lifecycle acceptance. The expanded physical batch still needs the new isolated PostgreSQL lifecycle gate before activation.

## Manual priorities and local policy

Prioritize Plaguepardos, Kaníbola, Kanívoras, Plantiguardias, Cresta Helada, Fuente del Mal, Tumba de Pandala and Or'Hodruin: these eight new candidates adapt reviewed guide room counts to proved native physical regions, using the same explicit DEV policy as teleport candidates. Reviewed final boss and native species pool are retained; original guide rooms remain verbatim in EXPANDED-PHYSICAL-COMPOSITIONS.json. This does not certify official encounter quantities.

For each, verify party arrival, reachable first encounter, closed forward gate before victory, opening afterward, every subsequent room, final exit, member rejoin and fresh reentry. Also prioritize four-room teleport Castillo de los Cangwejos and Torre Helada.

## Blocking gates

- **Kaskarlusca (native:34):** room 3 anchor `[-37,-11,4]` fails combat formation: `Dungeon anchor has no safe reachable combat terrain`. Rooms 1, 2 and 4 pass. Need a reachable native same-room combat area for the complete encounter and six players; do not silently relocate outside the room.
- **Sliptorium:** `No unique free native exterior exit for room 3`. Need literal source, approach, unconditional free destination and final-room binding. Command-based exit does not certify native exit.
- **Missing boss identity:** need reviewed role-to-native-ID evidence; name matching or level ranking is insufficient.
- **Unresolved species:** resolve every reviewed monster to installed native ID before roster compilation.
- **Unproved flow:** mixed transports, branches and open geometry require native ordering and victory unlock evidence; walkable floor alone does not prove progression.
- **Missing exterior entry:** exact route and arrival must be proved before instancing.
