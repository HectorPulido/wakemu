## Complete system acceptance

1. Leave old instances with `!exitdungeon`; group three ordinary players and place everyone at the selected exterior.
2. Enter through the native entrance. Everyone loads the same instance and sees each other and an accessible first group.
3. Next doors/transports reject crossing before victory; starting combat shows normal placement and turn order.
4. Win every stage. Exactly one next encounter appears; physical gates visibly open and permit crossing, or native transports move the active party to the correct room and floor.
5. A nonleader exits midway and reenters: same instance, no relocation of members who stayed.
6. Finish, receive rewards once, use the native exit, and reenter together: fresh instance and first encounter.
7. Repeat with assigned heroes; removed heroes stay out, surrender cleans up, and a subsequent fight starts. Spell behavior remains a separate workstream.

Ten singleton ultimate-boss arenas must each contain one initial boss. Torre Mineral and Kali each preserve four initial enemies. Tofullero solo basics are already accepted. [Earlier detailed risk checks](ACTIVATION-MANUAL-RISK-20261010.md).
