## Exact route and optional-content policies

- Kokolost retains three ordinary encounters plus its boss. The guide’s extra archmonster branch is optional, not a fifth mandatory room. No archmonster actors or initial summons are spawned. Its native annex terrain remains empty and within the relevant unlocked stage.
- Conohos native Door35992 has no adjacent walk-component pair in the verified geometry and is not on the mandatory route. This unresolved decorative/unreachable object is preserved as explicit audit evidence; no gate connection is fabricated.
- Conohos has two alternative entries/routes sharing the same boss. This candidate implements the four-room exterior route from map1134, not a fabricated six-room chain combining the mine branch. Mine shortcuts 35990/35991 are explicitly disabled. The reviewed final group has seven actors and a lower native-minimum score than the preceding eight-actor encounter; the local candidate adds one ordinary 4512 in the final room to satisfy the user’s increasing-composition requirement. This adjustment is recorded in the compiled plan.
- Jerry’s optional item-gated secret and its return transport are disabled; they are outside the mandatory three-room spine.
- Morbax retains its reachable annex terrain, but disables its foreign map1210 exit because that return contract is outside the reviewed map1137 entrance route. The mandatory final exit remains the literal native map1137 destination.
- Escarantera’s one-way disconnected transport source is unreachable from the mandatory route and is explicitly disabled. Its three mandatory guide encounters are preserved.

Disabled sources use the mixed runtime schema `disabled_native_sources`: exact source ID, nonempty reason and SHA256 of the complete native catalog source. They cannot overlap a reviewed mandatory door or exit. They hydrate hidden/unusable, and both internal and external travel reject them. No broad ignore rule is added.
